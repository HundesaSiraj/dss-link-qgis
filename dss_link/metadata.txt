about=DSS Link brings HEC-DSS time series into an interactive QGIS workflow and automatically combines D-part date blocks into one logical series. Quick start: (1) load a point station layer; (2) open DSS Explorer and use Setup to choose the DSS file, station layer, station ID field and matching DSS pathname part, usually B; (3) connect and scan; (4) use Explore > Activate station click tool and click a map station once; (5) inspect the graph, hover for date/value details, open the sortable Table or export the series to CSV; (6) use Catalog to filter C/E/F and select a logical series; (7) use Export DSS to linked GeoPackage for a reusable temporal GIS layer. Requires pydsstools in the Python environment used by QGIS (see the README). A synthetic sample DSS file and station layer are provided in the GitHub repository. Concept originator and lead: HUNDESA SIRAJ MOHAMMED — Remote Sensing Specialist, Climate Data Analyst, Hydrogeologist and WebGIS Expert. Contributor: MARTA FIKADU SHIKUR — Remote Sensing Specialist and Extreme Event Modeller with experience in SMEV, GEV and related extreme-value methods.
